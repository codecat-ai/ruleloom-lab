# Code of Conduct

Ruleloom Lab is a small education project. Participation should help people learn, experiment, and collaborate without hostility.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful when giving feedback.
- Explain technical disagreements with specific evidence.
- Welcome beginners and assume questions are asked in good faith.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Repeated off-topic comments after maintainers ask for focus.
- Publishing private information without clear permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, reviews, or releases.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; reject pull requests; or block participants when needed to protect the project. Reports can be made through a private maintainer contact if one is listed for the repository, or through GitHub reporting tools.
